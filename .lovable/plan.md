# Why the search result still shows a grey globe

I checked the live site and everything Google needs is already there and working:

- The icon files load correctly: `/favicon.ico` (256x256), `/logo.png` (512x512), `/apple-touch-icon.png` (180x180).
- The homepage's icon links are present in the served page, not just in the editor.
- The official logo markup (Organization) is live.
- `robots.txt` allows Google to fetch everything, including the icon files.

So nothing is broken on your side. The grey globe in the result you screenshotted is Google's own cached icon. Google fetches site icons with a separate crawler on its own schedule, and that cache typically refreshes a few days to about two weeks after it re-crawls the homepage. It cannot be forced from the site's code.

Also worth noting: the result in your screenshot is a product page, but Google takes the icon from the homepage only. So the homepage is the page that needs re-crawling.

## Small improvement I can still make

Google's own guidance prefers a square icon whose size is a multiple of 48px. Your current mark is 512x512, which is not. I would:

1. Add a `public/favicon-96.png` (96x96, a multiple of 48) generated by downscaling the existing green "g" mark — same artwork, no design change.
2. Point the homepage icon links at it, keeping `favicon.ico` as the fallback for older clients.

This is a nicety, not a fix — it slightly increases the chance Google renders the icon crisply once it does refresh.

## What you need to do (Google's side)

1. Publish, so the icon tweak goes live.
2. In Google Search Console, run URL Inspection on `https://www.gadgetopedia.shop/` (the homepage) and click **Request indexing**.
3. Wait. The icon appears once Google re-crawls and refreshes its icon cache — usually a few days, sometimes up to two weeks.

## Technical details

- New asset: `public/favicon-96.png`, produced with `magick public/logo.png -resize 96x96` (padded, never stretched).
- `src/routes/__root.tsx` head `links`: replace the `512x512` PNG icon entry with `{ rel: "icon", type: "image/png", sizes: "96x96", href: "/favicon-96.png" }`; keep the `.ico` entry, the apple-touch-icon, and the Organization JSON-LD unchanged.
- No other files, routes, or metadata change.
